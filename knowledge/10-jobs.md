# Jobs: choosing a class, 2nd job branches, builds

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Skill numbers come from CBT2 client data and may have
changed at launch. Sources: https://mscwhub.com/en/guides/class-tier-list ,
https://mscwhub.com/en/guides/warrior-branches , /magician-branches , /bowman-branches ,
/thief-branches , /ap-build-guide , /third-job . Patch changes are in 05-patches.md.

Context: no 3rd job at launch, so players live in their 2nd job for a long time.
Pick for the 2nd-job playstyle the player enjoys, not for 3rd-job hype.

## Quick picker (when the player hasn't chosen)
- Mob fast, easiest overall: Ice/Lightning Wizard (Magician)
- Safest leveling, little potion spend: Assassin (Thief), Cleric (Magician)
- Highest single-target damage: Bandit, Assassin (Thief)
- Always wanted in parties: Cleric; Fighter (party ATK buff)
- Range and safety: Hunter or Crossbowman (Bowman)
- Tanky, forgiving melee: any Warrior; Fighter has the best MP efficiency
- Makes the most mesos while leveling: Bandit (Steal)

## 2nd-job power tier list (community, CBT2 data, Sept 2026) (medium)
- S: Ice/Lightning Wizard, Assassin
- A: Bandit, Fire/Poison Wizard, Crossbowman
- B: Hunter, Cleric
- C: Fighter, Spearman, Page
Gaps are small without 3rd job; v1.1.0 buffed Thunder Bolt and Poison Breath.

## Warrior (advance in Perion) - main stat STR
- Fighter: swords or axes (axes favored: Axe Mastery adds Bleed). Rage = party ATK buff (up to +40). Ultimate Attack hits up to 3. Low difficulty, best party Warrior.
- Page: one-handed sword or blunt weapon (blunt favored: Blunt Mastery adds crit damage up to +20%). Threaten slows and lowers DEF of up to 4 monsters. Cheapest weapons. Defensive.
- Spearman: spear (DEF ignore, best for leveling) or polearm (move speed). Longest melee reach; Iron Will = knockback resist; Final Attack heals (capped 15% Max HP since v1.1.0). Medium difficulty.
- Future 3rd jobs: Crusader / White Knight / Dragon Knight (community favorite: Dragon Knight).

## Magician (advance in Ellinia) - main stat INT
- Ice/Lightning: Thunder Bolt hits up to 6 (best AoE), Cold Beam single target + slow; switch skills on lightning-resistant monsters. Teleport. Top tier.
- Fire/Poison: Fire Arrow single target + burn; Poison Breath DoT splash up to 6. Good boss damage. Switch to poison vs fire-resistant mobs.
- Cleric: Holy Arrow single target, Heal (party heal + damages undead), Bless. Weakest solo damage but lowest potion cost and always in demand. Great on undead maps (Wraiths, Zombie Mushrooms).
- All magicians burn MP: Orange/Lemon are the cheapest MP (1 MP per meso). Max Magic Guard early.

## Bowman (advance in Henesys) - main stat DEX
- Hunter (bow): Arrow Bomb AoE up to 6 + stun; faster attacks; best ranged mobbing.
- Crossbowman (crossbow): Iron Arrow pierces up to 6 in a line; Crossbow Mastery crit damage; higher per-hit and boss damage.
- Soul Arrow removes ammo cost for 60s. High-tier bows/crossbows mostly come from Woodworking crafting.

## Thief (advance in Kerning City) - main stat LUK
- Assassin (claw + throwing stars): ranged, auto-lock, Drain lifesteal, crits restore HP/MP. Very safe. Stars cost mesos (Claw Mastery can recover some).
- Bandit (dagger): Savage Blow up to 6 hits (top single target), Steal for extra loot. Melee, riskier at 30-40, most profitable Thief.
- Both get Haste (party speed buff). Choose Double Stab (dagger) OR Lucky Seven (claw) in 1st job, not both.

## AP builds (medium)
- Starting stats: 25 total, 12 in main stat. 5 AP per level.
- Warrior: all STR; add DEX only if missing often or for weapon requirements (new players: about 40-60 DEX by Lv 30).
- Magician: all INT; LUK only for gear requirements.
- Bowman: all DEX; STR only to equip the next bow.
- Thief: all LUK; DEX for accuracy/weapon requirements.
- Rule of thumb: frequent MISS = add accuracy stat. Avoid "-less" builds as a beginner. AP resets are costly.

## SP notes (medium)
- 2nd job gives about 121 SP, but maxing every 2nd-job skill needs about 140 - plan which skills to leave unmaxed.
- Warrior 1st job: max Power Strike and Iron Body. Magician 1st job: Magic Guard + main attack. Bowman: Double Shot / Critical Shot. Thief: Double Stab or Lucky Seven.

## Known bad info to ignore
Some guides list wrong job trees (e.g. "Hero at Lv 60", "Marksman at Lv 30", "Arch Mage at Lv 60"). In Classic World the 2nd job is the last job for now.
