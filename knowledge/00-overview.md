# MapleStory Classic World - overview and rules

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Confidence per line: high / medium / low.
Main source: MSCWHub guides (https://mscwhub.com/en/guides), which cite Nexon's FAQ (2026-09-29),
release notes and maintenance notices. Facts are paraphrased; see the linked pages for details.

## What it is
- Standalone client by Nexon, separate from modern MapleStory and from MapleStory Worlds. Installed via Nexon Launcher (toggle "MapleStory Classic World" under MapleStory) or Steam (install MapleStory, then the Classic World option; link Nexon account to Steam first). (high)
- Free-to-play. Founder's Access Oct 6 2026, Grand Launch Oct 21 2026. Windows and macOS. (high)
- Recreates pre-Big Bang MapleStory ("a parallel world where Big Bang never happened") with balance changes, reworked skills, a re-tuned EXP curve (about 1.25x the old table from Lv 30, steeper after Lv 90), crafting, and new content. It is NOT a byte-for-byte copy: old-game knowledge can be wrong here. (high)
- Level cap: 100. (medium - announced; CBT2 also capped at 100)
- Languages: English official, Spanish beta. Option to always show item names in English. (high)
- Controllers supported: DualSense, DualShock, Xbox (enable in options). (high)
- You cannot run Classic World and modern MapleStory at the same time. (high)

## Classes
- Four classes only: Warrior, Magician, Bowman, Thief (plus Beginner). NO Pirate, no modern classes. (high)
- 1st job at Lv 10, 2nd job at Lv 30. NO 3rd job at launch; Nexon said 3rd job and regions may come later and in a different order than the original. 4th job not available. (high)
- 1st job advancement has no stat requirement in Classic World (the old "20 INT / 25 DEX / 25 LUK" rules do not apply). (medium - one source; some low-quality guides still state the old rule)
- Job advancement towns: Warrior - Perion, Magician - Ellinia, Bowman - Henesys, Thief - Kerning City. (high)

## World at launch
- Open: Maple Island, Victoria Island (Henesys, Ellinia, Perion, Kerning City, Lith Harbor and surroundings), Sleepywood. (high)
- Not open at launch: Orbis, El Nath, Ludibrium (no LPQ), Forgotten Hollow (new Lv 39-59 zone, tested in CBT1, "coming in a future update"). Verify with web search before sending the player there. (high for launch state; may have changed since)
- Launch bosses: Mano, Mushmom, Shade, Zombie Mushmom, Jr. Balrog. Zakum is NOT in the game ("Zakum" is only a Founder's Package name). (high)
- Party Quest: Kerning City PQ only (channels 3-12 since v1.0.0). Henesys PQ and Ludibrium PQ not available. (high)
- Free Market with 20 rooms (no Auction House). 40 channels on world Windia. (high)
- Gear enhancement: legacy scrolls only (no Starforce/Potential/Flames). A failed scroll can destroy the item. (high)

## Account rules (important for advice)
- Characters start at Lv 1; nothing carries over from modern MapleStory except the NX balance. (high)
- Bans are shared between Classic World and modern MapleStory. (high)
- Trading unlocks after reaching Lv 15 and then waiting 24 hours. (medium)
- Debt System (since Oct 22): accounts caught in real-money trading owe a meso debt; until repaid they cannot trade, gift, open stores, drop items, or (since v1.1.0) pick up other players' drops. Never advise RMT or buying mesos. (high)
- Megaphones need Lv 15; whispers need Lv 10. (high)
- Citizenship system unlocks at Lv 12: town rank discounts on shops/storage (5% at first rank up to 50% at "Citizen of Honor"). (medium)
