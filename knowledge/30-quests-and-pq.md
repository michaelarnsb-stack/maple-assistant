# Quests and Party Quests

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Sources: https://mscwhub.com/en/guides/leveling-1-30 ,
/money-making , /party-quest-guide . Classic World added new Victoria Island quests at launch
that are not listed here.

## Worth doing (medium)
- Jane and the Mushroom (Lv 20, Lith Harbor): about 2,000 EXP, mesos, random Intermediate weapon scroll (can be valuable).
- Shumi's Lost Sack (Lv 35, Kerning City Construction Site line): 10,000 mesos + 30 Mana Elixirs.
- Maple Island tutorial quests: free starting gear.

## Kerning City Party Quest - KPQ (high)
- 4 players, Lv 21+, no upper level limit, 30 min, NPC Lakelis in Kerning City, channels 3-12.
- Stages: 1 coupons (25 total) -> 2 rope puzzle (3 members on ropes) -> 3 platform puzzle -> 4 barrel passes (6) -> 5 King Slime (Lv 32, ~16,820 HP, splits into Slimes at 50% and on death) -> bonus stage.
- Reward: Companion's Magic Box from Cloto (earrings, earring scrolls, potions, ores). First clear: quest "Proof of Companionship" with an Intermediate earring scroll.
- Henesys PQ and Ludibrium PQ are NOT in the game yet.
