# Patch history

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Sources: https://metaroad.gg/maplestory-classic/news/maplestory-classic-world-october-29-patch-notes ,
https://classicmaplewiki.com/guides/patch-notes/ (both summarize Nexon's maintenance notices).
Anything newer than v1.1.0 is not recorded here: use web search for recent changes.

## v1.1.0 - Oct 29 2026 (high)
- Buffs: Focus (Archer) accuracy now 5-25; Poison Breath (F/P) damage 60-90 and fixed at Lv 10; Thunder Bolt (I/L) damage 50-80.
- Fixes: Rush (Fighter/Page/Spearman) range grows 130 -> 150 as described; Spearman Final Attack HP absorb capped at 15% of Max HP.
- Lower HP: Copper Drake, Tortie, Clang, Drake (new values unpublished).
- More drops in Victoria Island monsters (unspecified).
- Woodcrafting arrow conversion now 100 -> 50 per attempt (same 2:1 rate).
- Players in debt cannot pick up other players' drops. Whispers need Lv 10.
- Citizenship ranks 4-10 need less Contribution.
- Fixes: Free Market stuck bug, channel-change disconnect with Final Attack, Mushroom Garden now allows Store Permit, Citizenship dailies could be done twice.

## Oct 22 2026 - unscheduled maintenance (high)
- Debt System introduced (see overview).
- KPQ coupons/passes can be dropped by accounts that haven't met trade requirements.

## v1.0.0 - Grand Launch, Oct 21 2026 (high)
- 40 channels; KPQ on channels 3-12; new map The Murky Swamp II (Kerning City area); new Victoria Island quests; Free Market rooms 12-20.
- Megaphones need Lv 15.
- Warrior: Improved HP Recovery item bonus +20-50%; Iron Body Weapon DEF +20-40%.
- Magician: Improved MP Recovery item bonus +10-25%.
- Thief: Dark Sight now semi-transparent to all players.
- Fixes: Final Attack duration 60s for all jobs; Poison Breath double MP cost; 2nd job quest failing with full Use inventory.
