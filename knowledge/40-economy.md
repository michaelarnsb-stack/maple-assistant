# Mesos, potions, crafting

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Sources: https://mscwhub.com/en/guides/money-making ,
/crafting-guide . Market prices change constantly: never quote prices as facts.

## Potions (medium)
- Best HP per meso: Red Potion, Fried Chicken, Meat (about 2 HP/meso).
- Best MP per meso: Orange, Lemon (about 1 MP/meso).

## Making mesos (medium)
- Level while you earn: pick up all drops, stack quests.
- Sell to players, not NPCs: attack/magic attack scrolls (NPC price ~1 meso), throwing stars, ores, crafting catalysts, good-stat gear.
- Keep for crafting: Processed Leather, Fragment of Magic, ores, monster ETC items.
- Bandit Steal and Assassin star recovery reduce costs. Bowman Soul Arrow saves ammo.
- Don't plan income around boss drops; Mushmom respawn is slow.
- Free Market (20 rooms) for trading; trade unlocks at Lv 15 + 24h. RMT leads to the Debt System.

## Crafting (medium)
- Classic World-only system: 6 professions, 348+ recipes - Smithing, Alchemy, Carpentry/Woodworking, Tailoring, Cooking, Accessory crafting.
- Few recipes are profitable at NPC prices; profit comes from player demand. Alchemy (ore refining) is the easiest profit; Carpentry is the cheapest to level.
- Bowman high-tier weapons largely come from Woodworking.
