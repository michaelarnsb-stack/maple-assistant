# Training spots by level

Status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL (2026-11-06). Source: https://mscwhub.com/en/guides/leveling-1-30 ,
/leveling-30-60 , /party-quest-guide . Monster/map availability can change with patches.

## Mechanics (medium)
- Monsters respawn on a 7.56-second global tick; each spawn point has its own cooldown and cap. Killing faster than the map respawns gives no extra EXP - pick maps with enough spawns for your kill speed.
- Accept every quest for a map before grinding it (quest stacking).
- Party play gives bonus EXP.

## Lv 1-10: Maple Island
- Snail Hunting Ground (1-3), Mushroom Forest area: Blue Snails, Green Mushrooms (3-8), Pig Beach (8-10).
- Finish all Maple Island quests before leaving (free gear and mesos).

## Lv 10-20: Victoria Island
- Stumpy's Territory (10-13).
- Deep Forest (13-18): stack Stump, Slime, Green Mushroom, Horny Mushroom quests.
- Horny Mushroom maps (15-20): good EXP, monsters are aggressive.

## Lv 20-30
- Slime Forest (20-25).
- Kerning City Construction Site B1-B3 (20-28), Subway Tunnel (25-30, good for parties, drops ores).
- Golem's Temple Entrance (25-30).
- Kerning City Party Quest from Lv 21 (4 players, NPC Lakelis, channels 3-12): often the best EXP for 21-30.

## Lv 30-40
- Wild Boar Land (30-35, leather for crafting).
- Ant Tunnel (33-38): Zombie Mushrooms and ants, ores.
- Sleepywood Dungeon (35-39): Cold Eye, Jr. Boogie.
- Ant Tunnel / Kerning City Subway (Zombie Mushrooms, ghosts) is the common 30-50 route for all classes.

## Lv 40-60
- Kerning City Subway Wraith maps (40-50): best mainstream map; Wraiths are undead (Cleric bonus) and teleport close (bad for melee Bandits).
- Drake, Copper Drake, Tortie, Clang: Lv 45-50 monsters whose HP was lowered in v1.1.0 (map locations not verified for Classic World).
- Forgotten Hollow (39-59, new zone, entry quest "Zelya's Map" from Grendel the Really Old in Ellinia at Lv 39) - NOT open at launch. Only recommend if web search confirms it has opened.
- Orbis/El Nath/Ludibrium maps are NOT available - do not recommend them.

## Bosses available (high)
- Mano, Mushmom (about Lv 40, 1-1.5h respawn, can drop Ilbi Throwing-Stars), Shade, Zombie Mushmom, Jr. Balrog.
